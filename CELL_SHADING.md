# CELL_SHADING.md

**Speculative modeling only.**

## Temporary definition (used in the 117-scenario epilepsy run)

Four bands per independent circuit/focus:

- dark
- quiet
- active
- seizing

Shade intensity is derived from excitability, settledness, Levy weight, and local microenvironment fit. It drives the heat-trace and charts.

## Observations from the run

- Seizing band covered ~3% of brain-days but carried ~80% of event weight.
- Shade spikes on the seizure day and drops below prior baseline within ~6 days.
- As pure display: no change to quantitative outcomes.
- When allowed to feed the sponge: reduced seizure freedom by 1–3 points.

## Relation to the psilocin gate

The gate can act as the band-switch threshold. 5-HT1A-dominant / within-dose-window conditions favor the quiet band; high-dose or antagonist conditions favor active/seizing.
